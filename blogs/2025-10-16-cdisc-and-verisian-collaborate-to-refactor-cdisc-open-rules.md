CDISC announced a partnership with Verisian, a software company focused on clinical trial solutions, to refactor the CDISC Open Rules Engine (CORE). The collaboration aims to enhance performance, improve rule authoring flexibility, and accelerate development of CDISC Open Rules while supporting broader adoption of global clinical data standards.
